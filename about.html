Who We Are

A London cleaning company with roots in sustainability.

Founded in 2021, Green GLO started as a two-person team with a passion for green living and a frustration with harsh chemical cleaners. What began as a small domestic service in South West London quickly grew into one of the city's most trusted eco-cleaning brands.

Today, we're a fully BICSc-accredited team serving homes, offices, and rental properties across Greater London. Every cleaner is trained in our Green GLO method — a rigorous, eco-conscious approach that prioritises hygiene, safety, and respect for your space.

We're proud to be a Living Wage employer, use 100% non-toxic, biodegradable products, and offset our carbon footprint through local tree-planting partnerships. Cleaning shouldn't cost the earth — and with Green GLO, it doesn't.

What Drives Us

Our core values

Every decision we make is guided by three simple principles: people, planet, and pristine standards.

Eco-First Always

We use only non-toxic, biodegradable, and cruelty-free products. No harsh chemicals, no harmful residues — just a naturally fresh clean.

People Over Profit

Our cleaners are paid fairly, trained thoroughly, and treated with respect. Happy cleaners deliver better cleans — it's that simple.

Uncompromising Standards
